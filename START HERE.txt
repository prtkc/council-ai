COUNCIL: FIRST-TIME SETUP (Mac)

You need a Claude subscription, a ChatGPT plan that includes Codex, or both.
One is enough, because a model can work against itself.

1. Move this Council folder somewhere you will keep it, such as Documents.

2. Open the Terminal app.

3. Type   zsh   followed by a space. Do not press Return yet.

4. Drag "Start Council.command" from this folder into the Terminal window,
   then press Return.

5. If Council asks to download Node.js, type y.

6. Council opens in your browser. Under Claude or ChatGPT, click Install,
   then Sign in, and follow the steps in the browser. Then click
   Test connection.

Keep the Terminal window open while you use Council.

After the first time, you can use the same Terminal steps to start it again.
Double-clicking "Start Council.command" also works if you allow it under
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
